White rice

The classic white rice

White rice is a classic a recipe we should never be without. Its preparation is very simple and quick, with very few ingredients, but despite this, there are a few tricks to keep in mind to make it perfect.

Ingredients

  • Cup long-grain white rice
  • Cups water
  • Teaspoon salt
  • Clove garlic
  • Tablespoon mild-flavored oil

Steps

  1. The first step is to wash the rice twice in a deep bowl (preferably one that won't spill). Drain well and set aside.
  2. Next, place a pot over medium heat, add the oil and the garlic clove, and sauté for 1 minute.
  3. Remove the garlic clove and add the washed rice. Saute for 1 or 2 minutes, stirring constantly to prevent sticking.
  4. Then add the room-temperature water and salt. Adjust the seasoning to ensure it's to your liking and let it cook without stirring for 12 to 15 minutes after it comes to a boil.
  5. After this time, cover the pot for 2 minutes and turn off the heat. We check that it's fully cooked and fluff it with a fork, preferably, to loosen it up.
  6. We cover it to keep it warm until serving time.
