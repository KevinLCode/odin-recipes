Yucca with mojo

The caribbean delight

Yucca with mojo is the perfect and essential side dish for a stylish and Caribbean-inspired dinner. Its appealing white color and the soft texture achieved by boiling make it irresistible.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg of cassava
  • 5 lemons
  • ¼ cup of vegetable oil or other mild-flavored oil
  • 8 cloves of garlic
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. First, prepare the cassava. Peel, wash, and chop it into pieces. Set aside.
  2. Next, in a pot with enough boiling water and a pinch of salt, add all the cassava and cook over high heat for about 15 to 20 minutes, or until tender. This will depend on the quality and freshness of the cassava.
  3. After this time, remove from the heat and drain.
  4. Meanwhile, prepare the mojo sauce. First, thoroughly crush the garlic cloves.
  5. Extract the juice from the lemons and mix it in a bowl with the garlic, oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper to taste.
  6. Let the mojo rest for about 10 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
  7. Serve the yucca on a platter and add the mojo so that it covers it completely. It's now ready to enjoy.
