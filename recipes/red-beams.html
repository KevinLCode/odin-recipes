Red beams

Beans on their max power

This red bean stew recipe is incredibly delicious, so you can't miss it.

Ingredients

  • 500g red beans
  • 2L water
  • 2 pig's trotters
  • 1 medium chorizo ​sausage
  • 1 medium piece of bacon
  • 1L pumpkin
  • 1/2 bell pepper
  • 1/2 onion
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Soak the beans in 2 liters of water overnight.
  2. The next day, pour the beans and all the water into a pressure cooker, add the pig's trotters, and cook for 25 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, prepare the rest of the ingredients: chop the pumpkin into medium-sized pieces, and finely chop the bell pepper and onion.
  4. We also mince the garlic, and dice the bacon and chorizo. We set everything aside to make the sofrito.
  5. For the sofrito, we heat the oil in a pan, add the bacon pieces, and cook until golden brown.
  6. We remove the bacon and set it aside while we continue with the sofrito. In the same pan, we add the garlic, onion, and bell pepper and sauté for about 2 minutes.
  7. Then we add the chorizo ​​and cook for 1 more minute before adding the cumin and oregano. We mix well and turn off the heat.
  8. Once the beans are soft, we pour the sofrito and the pumpkin pieces into the pot along with the bay leaf, salt, and pepper. We stir, cover, and cook for another 10 minutes.
  9. After this time, uncover the pot and check that the squash is soft and the stew has reached your desired thickness.
  10. If you want it thicker, simply let it cook uncovered for a few more minutes until it reaches your desired consistency.
  11. Once ready, you can enjoy it with rice, meat or eggs, and salad.
